# Checker B, numbers and consistency: M2

Fresh checker, 2026-11-06, at commit `20c64aa`. Read-only, no model runs, no node connection. My scripts and reruns are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.

**Bottom line**

- **Most of the numbers reproduce.** The four scorecards are byte-identical, and every tool rerun gives its saved text exactly: 19 of 19 files, no differing lines.
- **Two tools compare the wrong diameter.** `tools/m3_wood.py` and `tools/m2_validation.py` use the model's wood diameter where Repola's figures are over bark. The earlier phases converted it (`KNOWN_ISSUES.md:268`, `:371`).
