# Plan: one path from the four designs

2026-11-11. Planner: Fable 5.2 (ID `claude-fable-5-2`). Built from the four designs and their four challenges in `reports/design/`. Read only: no model run, no test, no commit. This is the only file I wrote. CONFIRMED = I checked it in code or saved data today. SUSPECTED = I did not.

## In short

1. Tooling, a desk check and one control run can start now. No model switch is built until the maintainer answers decisions 1 and 2 (section 6).
2. The tooling comes first because three tracks need the same new columns, and the two biggest proposals can't be judged without them.
3. Then at most three model changes. Each is a switch that is off, tested alone against the control on the same seeds: status on height, a crown space rule (only if it survives the desk check), and the branch-wood rate, last.
4. I expect most arms to fail their rules (end of section 4). A fail is reported as a fail, and nothing is adjusted to rescue it.
5. What this round can surely deliver: a true list of what is tuned, a scorecard that can't print PASS on a tuned row, corrected public wording, and numbers nobody has saved before (each tree's leader light, wood in plot trees, carbon limb by limb).
6. After all of it, the header at 50 and 60 will still read "independent passes: 0 of 0", or 1 of 1 at 50 at best. Only measured stands at those ages, opened at their source, change that.

## 8. Where the tracks disagreed

1. Both model challenges want an untuned row to improve before adoption. The validation rule says a row used to choose a fix stops being independent. Settled: the spread rows at 45 are guide rows. That costs nothing at 50 and 60, where they were INFO already (one stand per age).
2. The earned design would not look at spread when adopting. I took the challenge's do-no-harm, and PROTOCOL 9's improvement rule.
3. The crown design expects wider crowns to spread diameters. Its challenge shows leaf area already spreads four times as much as crown width (0.26 against 0.07 at 50). So EC runs only if both pass alone.
4. Three tracks draw on the same room: 0.3 points of light and 0.21 of LAI at 60. Hence the order E, C2, B, each against the default of that moment.
5. Top height was read two ways, 0.1-0.4 m apart. The approved definition is used everywhere, in arm B and in Q40's "13-15 m" too.
6. Two designs added bark to tree-list DBH twice. Corrected: dominants' crowns are 0.65-0.85 of Hemery (0.81 / 0.85 at 50 / 60); Repola for the plots is 16.9 / 21.9 t/ha by Eq. 9 and 14.0 / 15.5 by A3 at 50 / 60.
7. DBH spread at 60: the earned challenge asks for do-no-harm there. The only stand near 60 reads below the model (0.12 against 0.15), so I set no direction at 60.
8. PROTOCOL 2 asks 5 plots for a verdict. This plan uses 20 to adopt and 5 only to stop an arm.
