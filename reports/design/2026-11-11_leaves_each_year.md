**What was wrong (CONFIRMED).** `leaf_instances()` drew each leaf's slope, roll, size share and days to turn from its node and its number on it, with no year. Seed 5, stand tree, the leaves on the same node a year on:

**What is real.** Sourced: each read at the publisher or the authors' repository, CONFIRMED unless marked.

- **Leaves on a short shoot:** 2.35-2.66 (SD 0.6), with a catkin or without; 150 shoots each, May and July, five trees 3-4 m tall, Finland. The model has 2.5. *Tuomi et al. 1982, New Phytol. 91:483, Table 1, p. 485.*
- **One year to the next:** a short shoot's end bud has three rudimentary leaves (their stipules are the scales), 1-3 foliage leaves, then the next bud's three; an axillary bud (a shoot's first, or after flowering) has one. Mature *B. papyrifera*; nothing found for *B. pendula*. *Macdonald & Mothersill 1983, Can. J. Bot. 61:3049, abstract only.* The 2/5 spiral is Caesar 1983, as the species file has it: SUSPECTED.
- **Tilt of the blade's plane, by protractor on its fall line:** 49 over the crown by leaf area; 30 in the shade crown, about 55 at the top. Adult trees 22-26 m tall, closed stands, Germany, summer. *Hagemeier & Leuschner 2019, Forests 10:265, sections 2.2 and 3.2, Table 6.*
- **The same, as the leaf normal's angle from straight up, in level photographs:** mature trees. Estonia (by the coordinates), May to September 2013: 1,604 leaves, mean 56.8, SD 19.2 (park 59.6, natural forest 49.0). A botanical garden in Stockholm, 11 October 2011: 81 leaves, mean 32.3, SD 21.2. The means are my arithmetic. *Chianucci et al. 2018, Ann. For. Sci. 75:50; its data and site table Tab.S1, doi:10.17632/4rmc7r8zvy.2.*

**Does the look file agree?** Both sources measure the tilt of the blade's plane. Ours, from `inclination_deg: [30, 70]` and the look's roll: mean 51.7, SD 11.0, 5-95 %: 34-69. In the game target's files: mean 49.9 at 50 and 51.8 at 60, SD 8.

Nothing on master makes a time-lapse, so nothing else passes it. **What depends on a leaf keeping its place.** Within one age, nothing: blades, leaf cards and far cards come from the same leaves, and the growth history files hold short shoots, not leaves. A game showing one stage for several years shows that stage's leaves every spring, on or off; it takes a stage a year to see the change, and a cross-fade then dissolves one set into the next, which is right.
