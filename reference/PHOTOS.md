# Reference photos

The model was compared by eye with nine photos of real silver birch while it was built: solitary trees, stand edges
and dense stands. They are openly licensed Wikimedia Commons files (CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0 or public domain),
each credited with its author, licence and source in `reference/CREDITS.md`. They were never part of any model
output.

They are in this folder as 1280 px downscales, each under its own licence. Notes in the species file and the
reports that name one (for example `reference/gullmarsskogen_stand_5.jpg`) refer to these files.

Any photo added here must be openly licensed (CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, or public domain) and credited in
`reference/CREDITS.md`.
