    How to trace

    1. Click the stem base, then click wherever the trunk changes direction, plus every few trunk-widths on straight stretches.
    2. Stop where you lose the main stem in the crown. If it splits into two equal stems, tick Forked and follow the thicker one.
    3. Skip trunks touching the photo edges. Wide lenses bend straight lines there.
    4. Trace model renders the same way and set them to Model.
